Night Bite 🦟

An auditory Psychomotor Vigilance Test (PVT) with a snoring roommate  ·  聽覺版打蚊子警覺測驗

Settings · 設定

How to play · 玩法

  • It is late at night. Your hand rests under a desk lamp. A mosquito is buzzing somewhere in the dark.
    深夜，你的手放在檯燈下，蚊子在黑暗中嗡嗡飛。
  • The moment the mosquito lands in the lamp light, press SPACE (or tap the screen) to swat it — as fast as you can.
    蚊子一停到燈光下的手上，立刻按空白鍵（或點螢幕）拍下去，越快越好。
  • Don’t swat before it lands — you will only slap your own hand.
    蚊子還沒停下就拍，只會拍到自己。
  • Keep your eyes on the lamp light. The session lasts 3 minutes.
    眼睛盯著燈光處，全程 3 分鐘。
  • The lights are off and you are trying to sleep. A mosquito is flying somewhere far away in the room, and your roommate is snoring.
    燈關了，你正要睡覺，蚊子在房間遠處飛，室友在打呼。
  • The moment the loud whine suddenly appears, press SPACE (or tap) to swat it — as fast as you can. Nothing changes on screen; use your ears only.
    一聽到突然出現的響亮「嗡——」，立刻按空白鍵拍下去。畫面不會有任何提示，只能靠耳朵。
  • Don’t swat before you hear it — you will only slap your own ear.
    還沒聽到就拍，只會打到自己的耳朵。
  • Use the laptop’s built-in speakers and keyboard. Bluetooth speakers or keyboards add delay and will distort your reaction times. The session lasts 3 minutes.
    請使用筆電內建的喇叭和鍵盤，藍牙喇叭或鍵盤的延遲會讓反應時間失真。全程 3 分鐘。
