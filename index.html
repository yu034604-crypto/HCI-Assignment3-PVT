Night Bite 🦟

A mosquito-swatting Psychomotor Vigilance Test (PVT)  ·  打蚊子警覺測驗

How to play · 玩法

  • It is late at night. Your hand rests under a desk lamp. A mosquito is buzzing somewhere in the dark.
    深夜，你的手放在檯燈下，蚊子在黑暗中嗡嗡飛。
  • The moment the mosquito lands in the lamp light, press SPACE (or tap the screen) to swat it — as fast as you can.
    蚊子一停到燈光下的手上，立刻按空白鍵（或點螢幕）拍下去，越快越好。
  • Don’t swat before it lands — you will only slap your own hand.
    蚊子還沒停下就拍，只會拍到自己。
  • Keep your eyes on the lamp light. The session lasts 3 minutes.
    眼睛盯著燈光處，全程 3 分鐘。
Reaction timeOutcome
before landing / < 100 ms🖐️ Slapped yourself — false start
100 – 249 ms💀 Squashed! Clean kill
250 – 499 ms🦟 Maimed — it limps away with one wing
≥ 500 ms🩸 Too late — it already bit you (lapse)

Use headphones or speakers — the buzz is part of the experience. Stay on this tab; switching windows will distort the timing.
